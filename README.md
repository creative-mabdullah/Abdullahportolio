# M Abdullah | Digital Portfolio

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Portfolio-Live-brightgreen)](https://m-abdullah-digital.vercel.app/)

---

## Overview

This is the personal portfolio of **M Abdullah**, a **SEO Expert, Ecommerce Specialist, Graphic Designer, Video Editor, and Web Developer**.  
The website showcases professional skills, projects, and digital services offered.

Designed with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, it is fully responsive, fast-loading, and optimized for a modern digital presentation.

---

## 🔹 Features

- **Responsive Design:** Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.  
- **SEO Optimized:** Proper meta tags, keywords, Open Graph & Twitter Cards for social sharing.  
- **Portfolio Sections:**  
  - About Me  
  - Services & Expertise  
  - Projects & Case Studies  
  - Contact Form  
- **Interactive UI:** Smooth scrolling, modern fonts, and icon integration with Font Awesome.  
- **Cross-Platform Friendly:** Compatible with all major browsers.  

---

## 🛠 Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript  
- **Fonts & Icons:** Google Fonts (Unbounded), Font Awesome  
- **Hosting / Deployment:** [Vercel](https://vercel.com/)  
- **SEO & Social Media Preview:** Open Graph, Twitter Cards, Meta Tags
